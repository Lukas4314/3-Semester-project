\section{Conclusion}
In conclusion, a functional voice-controlled robot was developed, and was somewhat capable of localising sound sources using GCC-PHAT. MQTT worked sufficiently for real-time data transfer of audio data, despite not being designed for that purpose. The sound localisation system achieved an angle error of approximately 16 degrees under optimal conditions, although there is room for improvement in accuracy through higher sample rates and better microphone quality.
