\section{identifying commands}
(I think this should be placed after the whisper section)

When whisper returns a string of what has been said, the commands need to be extracted from the text so the robot can interpret what action has to be taken. This is done by processing the text (removing special characters like: ,.!) and searching for keywords, homophones (hear and here), or other words that sound similar (30 and further). All number words under 11 are also converted to numbers (one becomes 1), as well as units are converted to numbers (centimeter becomes a factor of 0.01).

The extracted command-words are split into different sections: action, direction, distance and unit. An action has to match a direction (drive and forward match, however turn and forward do not). Additionally, distance has to match the unit, driving a certain distance is measured in meters, while turning is measured in radians.

If the action is "come" and the direction is "here" the robot triangulates where the person speaking is standing and calculates the amount the robot has to turn and drive to move to the person speaking.
