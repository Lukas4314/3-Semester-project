\section{Testing}
Logger files are created during tests using the logger interface, where the raw files can be seen in \cref{app:testdata}. The interface generates a CSV file in a directory, where each column represents different data. Before writing to the file, the data is temporarily stored in a buffer. Standard values are set in the buffer to ensure that all fields contain data. The data is then overwritten and saved before being sent to the file.

A video of the robot playing against a player can be seen in \cref{app:testvideo}

Based on the tests the following was concluded:

The camera found the correct move made by the player $98.7\%$ of the time. The robot had a success rate of $97\%$. The gripper managed to pick up its own chess pieces $97.2\%$ of the time. It is important to note that castling counts as two pickups from the gripper. The camera managed to find the corners of the chessboard, every time including the pegs. The problem arose when the camera needed to detect the player's moves. The camera detected correctly on the first try $92.7\%$ and got an additional $6\%$ correct by verifying the legality of the move. In total only $3$ moves by the player were not registered correctly out of $233$ player moves.
